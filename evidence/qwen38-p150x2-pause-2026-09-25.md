# Qwen P150x2 service paused on britton-desktop

Date: 2026-09-25

## Change

`machines/britton-desktop/configuration.nix` sets
`systemd.services.qwen38-p150x2.enable = false`, which masks the unit and
leaves its definition in place. The machine check now requires the mask
instead of a `multi-user.target` start. The host Tenstorrent guide and
`AGENTS.md` record the pause and explain how to restore the service.

Commits: `eeccc43e` on the working branch, carrying the
`pause-qwen38-p150x2` change, and `76fdacba` on `add-omnibin-desktop`
(parent `b8d16f85`).

## Why it was needed

At 07:53 a pi session in `tenstorrent.nix/.pi/tmp/gdn-step` ran
`sudo systemctl stop qwen38-p150x2.service`. The omnibin activations at
09:41 and 09:45 started the service again through `multi-user.target`, and it
loaded the model onto both P150 cards. It was stopped at the operator's
request. Stopping it only lasts until the next boot or deployment, because
the unit is wanted by `multi-user.target`, by
`mesh-llm-mesh-llm-private-inference.service`, and by that unit's research
HTTP companion.

## Restoring

Delete the `enable = false` line, revert the machine check assertion, deploy,
and record a change that restores the boot-time requirement.
`ttwkv7-owner-control restore` and `systemctl unmask` cannot start the service
while the NixOS configuration masks it.
