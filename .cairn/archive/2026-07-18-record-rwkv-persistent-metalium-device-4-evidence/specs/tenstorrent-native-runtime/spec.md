## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Preserve terminal persistent Metalium device-4 evidence without collapsing process and orchestration outcomes

r[onix.tenstorrent.native_runtime.rwkv_lab.ttwkv7_persistent_metalium_device_4_evidence]

The system SHALL preserve the immutable device-4 evidence and validate that one physical process completed 24 accepted production ttWKV7 calls with 12 exact retained-state continuity edges, one response-isolated connection, matching numerical/ranking authorities, complete Inspector commits, and restored healthy ownership. It SHALL separately preserve that the enclosing pueue task and runbook returned status 1 because an obsolete flat receipt-field check failed after the physical process returned 0. It SHALL NOT rerun the terminal attempt, hide either status, or inflate the evidence into exact BF16 parity, fully device-resident execution, serving, throughput, latency, or general P150 compatibility.

#### Scenario: Device-free evidence validation accepts the immutable terminal fixture

- **GIVEN** the immutable package-owned terminal device-4 fixture
- **WHEN** the fixture and checker are evaluated without physical device access
- **THEN** the checker validates all 24 canonical request/response frames, finite BF16 values, request-bound authority, call order, 12 byte-exact continuity edges, 24 Inspector commits, numerical pass markers, matching token-2 rankings, restored owner health 200, healthy boards, inactive rollback, physical process status 0, classifier outcome `passed`, and orchestration status 1

#### Scenario: Evidence or interpretation drift is rejected

- **GIVEN** the immutable package-owned terminal device-4 fixture and its exact claim boundary
- **WHEN** framing, order, request identity, continuity, finite values, transcript length, classification, process status, post-process diagnosis, owner health, board status, workload commits, pueue status, file bytes, file count, or invocation shape changes
- **THEN** validation fails closed without opening a device or changing owner services
