## Why

The previous authorized orchestration never loaded because its committed runbook mode was `0644`. It retained invocation count zero and did not arm rollback, stop the owner, or access a Tenstorrent device. All fourteen Blackhole constant tiles therefore remain unmeasured.

## What Changes

- Create a fresh runbook committed as mode `100755` and prove both filesystem and Git-tree launchability before authorization can reach service isolation.
- Bind a new evidence root, Inspector port, rollback unit, reviewed commit, active closure, exact package/kernel outputs, owner helper, physical device 1, and one 180-second process timeout.
- Arm the independent root-systemd restoration timer before isolation and retain ordinary helper-based exit-trap restoration.
- Consume exactly one explicitly authorized `probe` process result without retry or alternate launch command.

## Impact

- **Files**: this Cairn change, an executable one-shot runbook, the accepted Tenstorrent runtime requirement after sync, and the completed archive.
- **Runtime**: at most one ttWKV7 probe invocation on physical device 1 after all device-free gates pass.
- **Service**: temporary owner stop with immediate and independently timed restoration.
- **Non-goals**: no deployment, source repair, full-WKV execution, tolerance change, alternate device, fallback command, retry, or general Blackhole compatibility claim.
