# Record the terminal persistent Metalium device-4 evidence

## Why

The one archived device-4 runbook invocation is terminal. Its physical process completed 24 canonical ttWKV7 calls and the classifier recorded `passed`, while the enclosing runbook returned status 1 because its post-process validator expected obsolete flat receipt fields. The complete result must be preserved without rerun, schema repair, or claim inflation.

## What changes

- Preserve the immutable device-4 transcript, receipts, Inspector records, diagnostics, restoration evidence, and pueue observation outside the ordinary harness closure.
- Add a pure Rust verifier for framing, request/response authority, finite BF16 payloads, exact retained-state continuity, workload commits, numerical receipt authorities, board health, owner restoration, and the post-process mismatch.
- Add positive and adversarial Nix checks that reject evidence, framing, authority, continuity, health, workload, count, and file-set drift.
- Record the narrow physical success and the separate orchestration failure as simultaneous terminal facts.

## Non-goals

- No hardware rerun, reconnect, fallback, owner-service change, board query, device open, or kernel execution.
- No exact BF16 parity, fully device-resident layer/model, serving, throughput, latency, or general P150 compatibility claim.
- No reinterpretation of terminal tasks 30, 64, 281, or 25.
